Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

Floodline Ark is designed as a simple offline puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Floodline Ark does not require an account, does not ask for personal information, and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, photos, contacts, or messages.

Children's Privacy

The game is designed to be approachable for young players and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Offline Play and Local Progress

Game progress and settings may be saved locally on the device so players can continue where they left off. This information stays on the device and is not sent to a server by the app.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, online leaderboards, or cloud accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
